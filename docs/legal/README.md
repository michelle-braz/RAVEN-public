# Legal position

> Classification: Legal. This is an engineering statement of what exists, not legal advice. Nothing here is a
> compliance claim.

## What the repository provides

* A proprietary, all-rights-reserved notice ([LICENSE](../../LICENSE)); licence history and third-party
  obligations in [licensing](../licensing/LICENSING.md).
* Technical measures relevant to security and privacy duties: [security model](../security/security-model.md),
  [data handling](../privacy/data-handling.md) (export, retention and erasure tools, no third-party sharing).
* Honest capability statements: [product overview](../product/overview.md). The product does not promise
  accuracy, uptime or certification.

## What requires counsel or a business decision (not in this repository)

| Item | Why it is needed before charging customers |
|---|---|
| Customer agreement (subscription or licence terms, acceptable use, liability limits, warranty disclaimer, support and availability terms) | The LICENSE grants nothing by itself; customers need a signed grant |
| Data processing agreement and roles (processor or controller), legal basis, international transfers | Customers may send personal data in incident text |
| Privacy notice for the vendor's own processing (support contacts, billing) | Separate from the software |
| Breach-notification commitments and contacts | Contractual and regulatory |
| Trademark clearance for the names "RAVEN" and "FOXHUMAN" | Common names; not checked here |
| Confirmation of the MIT-to-proprietary transition and of ownership of AI-assisted code | See [licensing](../licensing/LICENSING.md) |
| Repository visibility decision | The source is currently public; see licensing |
| Security contact and vulnerability-handling commitments | See [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) |
